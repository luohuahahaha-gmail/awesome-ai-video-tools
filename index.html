🔍 How to Choose an AI Video Tool

When selecting an AI video generator, consider the following factors:

  • Use case: Image animation, text-to-video, avatar creation, or video editing each require different tools.
  • Output quality: Check resolution support (720p, 1080p, 4K) and video length limits.
  • Speed: Generation time varies from seconds to minutes depending on the model and queue.
  • Pricing: Many tools offer free tiers with limited credits. Compare cost-per-generation for heavy usage.
  • API access: If you need to integrate AI video into your own product, look for tools with developer APIs.
  • Commercial license: Confirm usage rights if you plan to use generated videos commercially.
